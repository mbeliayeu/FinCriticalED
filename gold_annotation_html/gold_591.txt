than interest.</p><p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0"> </p><p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">Gain or loss recognized by
a U.S. Holder on a sale or other taxable disposition of the notes generally will constitute capital gain or loss and will be long-term
capital gain or loss if the notes were held by the U.S. Holder for more than one year. For non-corporate U.S. Holders, the
net amount of long-term capital gain generally will be subject to taxation at reduced rates. A U.S. Holder’s ability to offset
capital losses against ordinary income is limited. Gains recognized by a U.S. Holder on a sale or other taxable disposition of the
notes generally will be treated as U.S.-source income for U.S. foreign tax credit purposes.</p></body></html>
