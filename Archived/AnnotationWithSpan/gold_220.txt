Special rules, which are not discussed below, may apply to a non-resident of Canada that is an insurer which carries on business in Canada
and elsewhere. This portion of the summary assumes that no interest paid on a Non-Resident Note Holder’s notes will be in respect
of a debt or other obligation to pay an amount to a person with whom BFI or the Company does not deal at arm’s length within the
