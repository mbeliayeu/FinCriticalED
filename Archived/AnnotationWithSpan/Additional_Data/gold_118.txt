 <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman" align="center"><b><a name="tx764677_24"/>Maturity Considerations </b></p>
<p style="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman" align="justify">The pooling and servicing agreement and the Series <font style="white-space:nowrap">2025-1</font> supplement for this series
provide that the Class A certificateholders will not receive payments of principal until the Expected Final Payment Date, or earlier in the event of a <font style="white-space:nowrap">Pay-Out</font> Event which results in the commencement of
the Early Amortization Period. Class A certificateholders will receive payments of principal on each Special Payment Date until the Class A Invested Amount has been paid in full or the Series <font style="white-space:nowrap">2025-1</font>
Termination Date has occurred. The Class B certificateholders will not begin to receive payments of principal until the final principal payment on the Class A certificates has been made. The holder of the Collateral Interest will not begin
to receive payments of principal until the final principal payment on the Class B certificates has been made. </p> <p style="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman" align="justify">On
each Distribution Date during the Controlled Accumulation Period, amounts equal to the least of: </p> <p style="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; margin-left:4%; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman" align="justify">(a)
Available Principal Collections (<i>see</i> <i>Series Provisions  Principal Payments</i> <i>in this prospectus</i>) for the related Monthly Period on deposit in the Collection Account, </p>
<p style="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; margin-left:4%; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman" align="justify">(b) the Controlled Deposit Amount, and </p>
<p style="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; margin-left:4%; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman" align="justify">(c) the sum of the Class A Adjusted Invested Amount and the Class B Adjusted Invested Amount </p>
<p style="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman" align="justify">will be deposited in the Principal Funding Account for Series <font style="white-space:nowrap">2025-1</font> held by the trustee until the
Expected Final Payment Date or the first Special Payment Date. <i>See Series Provisions  Principal Payments in this prospectus for a discussion of the circumstances under which the commencement of the Controlled Accumulation Period
may be delayed.</i> </p> <p style="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman" align="justify">Subject to satisfaction of the Rating Agency Condition, the transferor may, at or after the time at
which the Controlled Accumulation Period begins for Series <font style="white-space:nowrap">2025-1,</font> cause the trust to issue another series (or some portion thereof, to the extent that the full principal amount of such other series is not
otherwise outstanding at such time) as a paired series with respect to Series <font style="white-space:nowrap">2025-1</font> to be used to finance the increase in the Transferor Amount caused by the accumulation of principal in the Principal Funding
Account with respect to Series <font style="white-space:nowrap">2025-1.</font> Although no assurances can be given as to whether such other series will be issued and, if issued, the terms thereof, the outstanding principal amount of such series may
vary from time to time (whether or not a <font style="white-space:nowrap">Pay-Out</font> Event occurs with respect to Series <font style="white-space:nowrap">2025-1),</font> and the interest rate with respect to certificates of such other series may
be established on its date of issuance and may be reset periodically. Further, since the terms of the Series <font style="white-space:nowrap">2025-1</font> certificates will vary from the terms of such other series, the <font style="white-space:nowrap">Pay-Out</font> Events or Reinvestment Events with respect to such other series will vary from the <font style="white-space:nowrap">Pay-Out</font> Events with respect to Series <font style="white-space:nowrap">2025-1</font>
and may include <font style="white-space:nowrap">Pay-Out</font> Events or Reinvestment Events which are unrelated to the status of the transferor or the servicer or the receivables, such as <font style="white-space:nowrap">Pay-Out</font> Events or
Reinvestment Events related to the continued availability and rating of certain providers of series enhancement to such other series. If a <font style="white-space:nowrap">Pay-Out</font> Event or Reinvestment Event does occur with respect to any
such paired series prior to the payment in full of the Series <font style="white-space:nowrap">2025-1</font> certificates and the Collateral Interest, the final payment of principal to the Series <font style="white-space:nowrap">2025-1</font>
certificateholders and the holder of the Collateral Interest may be delayed. </p> <p style="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman" align="justify">Should a
<font style="white-space:nowrap">Pay-Out</font> Event occur with respect to the Series <font style="white-space:nowrap">2025-1</font> certificates and the Collateral Interest and the Early Amortization Period begin, any amount on deposit: </p>
<p style="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; margin-left:4%; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman" align="justify">(a) in the Principal Funding Account will be paid to the Series <font style="white-space:nowrap">2025-1</font>
certificateholders on the first Special Payment Date, and the Series <font style="white-space:nowrap">2025-1</font> certificateholders and the holder of the Collateral Interest will be entitled to receive Available Principal Collections on each
Distribution Date with respect to such Early Amortization Period as described herein until the Class A Invested Amount, the Class B Invested Amount and the Collateral Invested Amount are paid in full or until the Series <font style="white-space:nowrap">2025-1</font> Termination Date occurs, and </p> <p style="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; margin-left:4%; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman" align="justify">(b) in the Special Funding Account
will be released and treated as Shared Principal Collections to the extent needed to cover principal payments due to or for the benefit of any series, including Series <font style="white-space:nowrap">2025-1</font> entitled to the benefits of Shared
Principal Collections. <i>See</i> <i>Series Provisions  <font style="white-space:nowrap">Pay-Out</font> Events</i> <i>in this prospectus</i>. </p>
